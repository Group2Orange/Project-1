Company Policies

Last updated: October 2025 · General workplace regulations, security, and standards

Welcome to the TeamSpace HR policy directory. Our policies exist to foster a safe, collaborative, and inclusive environment. They define our workplace ethics, leave allowances, distributed operations, and information security requirements across all departments.

1. Workplace Conduct & Ethics

We maintain unwavering standards for professional integrity, mutual dignity, and equal opportunity across the entire organization.

Equal Opportunity & Non-Discrimination: We do not tolerate discrimination or harassment of any form based on gender, race, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or veteran status.

Transparent Grievance Pathways: Immediate access to anonymous reporting via the HR Compliance Desk with guaranteed whistleblower non-retaliation.

Conflict of Interest: Employees must proactively disclose external commercial engagements or advisory activities that may interfere with their duties.

2. Working Hours & Attendance

Our scheduling accommodates diverse time zones while ensuring sufficient overlapping hours for responsive cross-team collaboration.

Core Collaborative Hours: Teams observe synchronized active availability between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM CST for key meetings and standups.

Annual Leave Accrual: Full-time staff accrue paid leave at a baseline rate of 1.75 business days per month.

Advance Notice: Planned absences exceeding 5 business days require a minimum 2-week advance submission.

3. Remote Work & Equipment

Distributed employees receive structured technological and ergonomic support to ensure productive, ergonomic home setups.

Ergonomics Allowance: A $750 reimbursement is eligible every 24 calendar months for desks, monitors, and certified ergonomic seating.

Company Hardware: Standardized workstation hardware remains corporate property.

International Remote Work: Relocations or remote tenures beyond 14 days outside your primary employment country require prior written HR approval.

4. Data Privacy & Security

Customer trust and regulatory compliance demand strict individual diligence across all devices and services.

Zero Trust Access: Mandatory two-factor authentication and clean-screen principles in public spaces.

Confidentiality: Proprietary data, source code, and customer records must never be uploaded to unapproved public AI tools or external cloud accounts.

Incident Escalation: Any suspected loss of device, credential leak, or phishing attempt must be flagged to security within 60 minutes.
